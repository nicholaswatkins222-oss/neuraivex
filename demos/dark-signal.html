// how it works

One system. Built around your ICP.
Tuned every Monday.

01 / signal
We define a target you can recognize.

Apollo, Clay, and intent signals (G2 visits, hiring posts, funding events) get filtered through your ICP. Every lead has a reason to be on the list.

02 / send
Personalized at the account level. Not at the variable level.

Claude reads each prospect's site, recent posts, and stack — then writes the first line. No {{firstName}} tricks. Reply rates that survive spam filters.

03 / iterate
Variants that don't book get killed by Friday.

Every Sunday, our analyzer auto-pauses any variant under 0.5% positive reply rate after 50 sends. The winners scale. The losers die quietly.
