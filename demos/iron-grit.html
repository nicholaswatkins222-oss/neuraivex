What We Do

Heavy-Duty
Roofing Systems
For Heavy-Duty Buildings.

We don't chase residential reroofs. Our crews work flat, low-slope, and standing-seam systems on the buildings that keep Denver running — distribution centers, manufacturing plants, retail boxes, and HOA flat-roof portfolios.

TPO Membrane Systems

60-mil and 80-mil mechanically-attached and fully-adhered TPO from Carlisle, GAF, and Versico. NDL warranties up to 30 years on qualifying installs.

Avg install: 9 days per 50,000 sq ft

Modified Bitumen (Mod-Bit)

Two-ply SBS and APP systems on warehouses, schools, and mid-rise multifamily. Self-adhered or torch-applied, granular cap sheet finish for UV durability.

23 schools re-roofed across CO & WY

Standing-Seam Metal

24-gauge Galvalume and Kynar-coated steel panels on retail, ag, and self-storage. We roll-form panels on-site to length — no field splices, no leaks.

40-yr panel + 20-yr finish warranty

Roof Coatings & Restoration

Silicone and acrylic coatings that extend an aging roof 10–15 years without the capital cost of replacement. Tax-deductible as repair on commercial books.

Avg savings: 62% vs full replacement

Storm & Hail Response

Tarp and dry-in within 4 hours of a hailstorm or wind event. We document for insurance, work directly with adjusters, and don't disappear when the deductible is signed.

96-hr avg insurance turnaround

Preventive Maintenance

Annual or bi-annual inspections, drain clearing, flashing repair, and sealant refresh. Quarterly reports per building. Keeps NDL warranties active.

Portfolios from 4 to 80 roofs
