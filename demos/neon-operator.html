Quick answers.

How fast can you actually get here?

Most Phoenix-metro pickups happen within 2 hours of booking. Sunday afternoons can stretch to 3 hours. If you book before 11 AM, we almost always have a same-day slot.

What won't you take?

Hazardous chemicals, wet paint, motor oil, asbestos, and live ammunition. We take just about everything else — including appliances, e-waste, hot tubs, and pianos.

Do I need to be home?

No. About 30% of our jobs are unattended — we confirm by photo before and after. Pay by text once you've reviewed the photos.

What service area do you cover?

Phoenix, Scottsdale, Tempe, Mesa, Chandler, Gilbert, Glendale, Peoria, and Surprise. Outside the 101/202 loops we add a small fuel fee — quoted upfront before booking.

Are you actually insured?

Yes — $2M general liability through Acuity. We can email a COI to your HOA or property manager within 30 minutes.
