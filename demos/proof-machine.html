Common questions

Things people ask before booking.

Do I need to provide water or electricity?

No. Our vans carry their own 60-gallon water tank, generator, and water-reclaim equipment. We can detail in apartment lots, condo garages, and HOA driveways without hookups.

What's your service area?

Portland metro, including Beaverton, Tigard, Lake Oswego, Hillsboro, Tualatin, Gresham, and parts of Vancouver, WA. Outside this radius we charge a flat $40 travel fee.

How long does a full interior take?

Most full interior restorations run 4–6 hours. We tell you in advance and don't leave until it matches the photos on this site.

What if I'm not happy with the result?

If anything doesn't match the package description, we come back within 7 days and re-do it at no charge. We've issued exactly 4 redos in the last 12 months.

Do you offer monthly maintenance?

Yes. Our subscription members get a discounted exterior + interior maintenance every 2 or 4 weeks, billed monthly. Most fleet customers move to this plan after their first detail.

Ready for the driveway treatment?

Book online in under 90 seconds. We confirm by text within the hour, show up exactly when we said we would, and leave the car cleaner than the day you bought it.
