FAQ

Things we get asked, before the demo call.

How long does setup actually take?
For most React, Next.js, and Vue apps, it's a single npm install and a one-line init call. Auto-capture starts flowing events the moment you deploy. For server-side or mobile, our SDK templates take an afternoon at most.
How is Pulse different from Mixpanel, Amplitude, or PostHog?
Three things: auto-capture is on by default (no manual instrumentation queue), session replay is included in every tier above Starter, and event volume — not seats — is the only thing that drives pricing. Most teams cut their analytics bill by 40–60% when they switch.
Do you have a free trial without entering a card?
Yes. Starter is free forever with 50k events/month and unlimited seats. If you want to evaluate Growth-tier features, the 14-day trial doesn't require a card either — we'll send a reminder before it expires.
Can I host this in our own cloud?
On the Scale tier, yes. We support customer-managed AWS and GCP deployments with the same UI, plus a single-tenant managed offering in EU and US regions. Talk to sales for an architecture call.
What does the migration from our current tool look like?
Most teams run Pulse alongside their current tool for 30–60 days. We'll import your existing event schema, mirror your top dashboards, and provide a side-by-side comparison. Our solutions engineer will run the kickoff at no extra cost.
